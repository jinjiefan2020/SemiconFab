⚠️ Engineering Assumptions & Methodology Caveats

  • Baseline Prototype Line vs. High-Volume GigaFab: The 3D Digital Twin cleanroom layout represents a balanced modular prototype line (121 tools across all 18 bays). In commercial high-volume manufacturing (30k–50k WSPM), dry etch and thin-film deposition tools scale significantly faster than EUV scanners to resolve process bottlenecks.
  • Statistical Extreme Stacking: Summing all minimums (Low) and all maximums (High) assumes correlated extremes across 100+ separate procurement negotiations. The Median Scenario represents the most mathematically robust baseline for engineering budget feasibility.
  • Infrastructure & Subfab Boundary: Facility shares (20%–35%) are anchored to verified industrial data from TSMC Arizona, Kumamoto Fab 1, and Samsung Taylor disclosures, covering civil shell, UPW, gases, high-voltage power, HVAC FFU, and AMHS monorails.
