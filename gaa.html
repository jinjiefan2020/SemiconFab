GAAFET 3D Nanosheet Transistor Fabrication Process: 10-Step 3nm/2nm Industrial Engineering Breakdown

This interactive digital twin details the complete industrial fabrication sequence of next-generation 3nm / 2nm Gate-All-Around Nanosheet (MBCFET / RibbonFET) transistors using Si/SiGe superlattice epitaxy and 360-degree all-around high-k metal gate wrapping.

10-Step GAAFET Nanosheet Semiconductor Fabrication Sequence

  1. Step 1: Silicon Substrate & Well Implantation (P/N-Well)

    Single-crystal 300mm silicon substrate preparation and retrograde well implantation providing the bulk silicon base for advanced sub-3nm nanosheet CMOS architectures.

    Equipment: High-Energy Ion Implanter. Materials: P-Type Si Substrate, Boron (p-well) / Phosphorus (n-well) dopants.

  2. Step 2: Si/SiGe Alternating Superlattice Epitaxy

    Atomic-precision chemical vapor epitaxy growing alternating layers of single-crystal silicon channels (~5nm) and sacrificial Si0.7Ge0.3 layers (~5nm) with atomic-level interface abruptness.

    Equipment: Ultra-High Vacuum CVD (UHVCVD) Epitaxy Reactor (Applied Materials Centura Epi / ASM Intrepid). Materials: Pure Silicon (Si) + Silicon-Germanium (Si0.7Ge0.3, 30% Ge).

  3. Step 3: EUV Fin Pitch Splitting & Superlattice Anisotropic Dry Etch

    Extreme Ultraviolet (0.33 NA EUV) lithography defining dense nanosheet fin pitches, followed by anisotropic plasma dry etching through all Si/SiGe layers with vertical sidewall angles (>89°).

    Equipment: EUV Lithography Scanner (ASML Twinscan EXE/NXE) + High-Density Conductor RIE (Lam Research Kiyo / TEL Tactras). Materials: Si/SiGe Multi-Layer Superlattice Fin Pillars.

  4. Step 4: Shallow Trench Isolation (STI) Oxide Fill, CMP & Recess

    Flowable CVD oxide trench filling, CMP planarization, and selective oxide recess exposing the multi-layer nanosheet fin active stack.

    Equipment: Flowable CVD (AMAT Producer) + CMP Polisher + Wet Recess Station. Materials: SiO2 STI Dielectric Oxide.

  5. Step 5: Dummy Poly-Si Gate Patterning & SiN Top Hardmask

    EUV patterning of sacrificial polysilicon dummy gate spanning orthogonally over the superlattice fin pillars, establishing the channel gate length.

    Equipment: EUV Scanner + Dry Plasma Poly Etcher. Materials: Polysilicon (~65nm) + Si3N4 Hardmask (~25nm).

  6. Step 6: Selective SiGe Lateral Cavity Recess & Inner Spacer Formation

    Isotropic gas-phase chemical etch selectively pulling back SiGe by ~5nm, followed by conformal ALD of low-k SiBCN/SiOCN inner spacers to minimize parasitic gate-to-drain capacitance.

    Equipment: Atomic-Precision Gas-Phase Dry Chemical Etcher + ALD System (TEL Certas / AMAT Producer). Materials: Low-k Dielectric Inner Spacers (SiBCN/SiOCN, k < 4.2).

  7. Step 7: Source/Drain Selective Epitaxy & Dopant Activation

    In-situ doped selective epitaxy growing faceted Si:P (nFET) or SiGe:B (pFET) touching all stacked nanosheet channel ends simultaneously, drastically reducing contact resistance.

    Equipment: UHVCVD Epitaxy Cluster (AMAT Epi / ASM). Materials: Heavily Doped Si:P (nFET, 3e21 cm⁻³) / SiGe:B (pFET, 50% Ge).

  8. Step 8: ILD0 Oxide Gap Fill & CMP Gate Uncovering

    Depositing interlayer dielectric oxide (ILD0) and chemical mechanical polishing down to uncover the dummy polysilicon gate top surface.

    Equipment: PECVD System + Advanced CMP System (AMAT Reflexion LK). Materials: Low-k SiO2 Interlayer Dielectric.

  9. Step 9: Dummy Gate Strip & Sacrificial SiGe Nanosheet Release

    Wet strip of dummy gate, followed by highly selective chemical vapor etching (selectivity > 150:1) removing all sacrificial SiGe layers to release completely suspended silicon nanosheets.

    Equipment: Gas-Phase Selective Dry Etch System (Tokyo Electron TEL Certas / Applied Materials Siconi). Materials: Gas-phase vapor chemistry / Dilute H2O2 wet solution.

  10. Step 10: 360-Degree All-Around HKMG Gate Metallization & Contact Routing

    ALD coating sub-nanometer interfacial oxide, HfO2 high-k dielectric, work-function metal stacks, and Tungsten gate fill completely surrounding each nanosheet wire in 360 degrees.

    Equipment: High-k Metal Gate ALD Cluster (TEL Trias / AMAT Olympia) + MOL Metallization. Materials: Interfacial SiO2 (~0.8nm) + HfO2 High-k (~1.4nm) + TiN/TaN Work-Function + W Fill.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) on GAAFET Nanosheet Fabrication

Why does GAAFET replace FinFET at 3nm and 2nm?
GAAFET surrounds the horizontal silicon nanosheet channels on all four sides (360 degrees) with the gate, restoring full electrostatic gate control, enabling higher drive currents per footprint, and supporting variable nanosheet widths for power/performance optimization.
What is the critical role of Inner Spacers in GAAFET architecture?
Inner spacers are nanoscale dielectric plugs (SiOCN/SiBCN) formed inside the lateral recesses left by sacrificial SiGe. They physically and electrostatically isolate the replacement metal gate from the heavily doped epitaxial source and drain, drastically reducing parasitic gate capacitance (Cgd/Cgs) which is essential for high-frequency switching.
How is nanosheet release achieved without damaging the silicon channels?
Nanosheet release relies on highly selective isotropic etching (using gas-phase vapor chemistry or hot chemical mixtures like H2O2/HF) that dissolves Si0.7Ge0.3 with an etch selectivity exceeding 150:1 over pure silicon, leaving the pristine 5nm-thin silicon nanosheets suspended without stiction or thickness loss.
