01 What is this?

Introduction and core project scope

01 这是什么

平台概览与核心定位

SemiconFab is a semiconductor factory digital twin that opens in your browser: walk into a 3D cleanroom of a 300mm wafer fab, an OSAT packaging plant, and an interactive 3,000-tool 3nm Mega Fab conceptual layout model; browse an open catalog of 253 real OEM tools, compare specs and price evidence, estimate what it costs to build a fab, and download an open benchmark for fab-scheduling research. It is a teaching- and research-grade tool — every data point carries an honest provenance label, and nothing pretends to be industrial-grade precision.

🌐
Languages: the homepage supports 9 languages; all other pages are English / 中文 bilingual (toggle in the top-right corner).

SemiconFab 是一个在浏览器里打开的半导体工厂数字孪生：你可以走进一座 300mm 晶圆厂的 3D 洁净室、一座封测厂，以及容纳 3,000 台机台的 3nm 概念超级晶圆厂布局模型；查阅 253 款真实 OEM 设备的开放目录， 对比参数与价格依据，估算建一座晶圆厂要花多少钱，还可以下载调度研究用的 公开基准测试。它是教学与研究级的工具——数据诚实标注来源等级， 不冒充工业级精度。

300mm Fab Twin 300mm Fab Twin

twin.html
The front-end wafer fab cleanroom 前道晶圆厂洁净室

A 3D twin of an advanced 300mm fab: the L1 process cleanroom (lithography, etch, thin film, Cu, implant, diffusion, CMP & clean, metrology bays) plus the B1 subfab below it (vacuum pumps, gas manifolds, UPW piping), running a live operations simulation (yield waterfall, tool OEE, AMHS logistics, MES / SECS-GEM telemetry). 一座 300mm 先进晶圆厂的 3D 双子：L1 工艺洁净室（光刻、刻蚀、薄膜、铜、注入、扩散、CMP、量测等 bay）+ B1 地下 subfab（真空泵、气体、超纯水管网），并带实时运行仿真（良率瀑布、设备 OEE、AMHS 物流、MES/SECS-GEM 遥测）。

  • Guided tour: click Tour in the top bar for a 5-stop cinematic tour (macro fab → litho bay → etch bay → AMHS → B1 subfab). Previous / Next / Skip work throughout; the tour stops after one full round. A 3-step onboarding bubble appears on first visit.
  • Camera & layers: presets L1 / B1 / Dual / Explode; layer toggles AMHS / FFU / Airflow / Heatmap / X-Ray (X-Ray opens an MEP piping see-through view, filterable by medium).
  • Inspect a tool: click a tool in the left list or in the 3D scene. The inspector shows its footprint (SEMI E72), weight, SECS/GEM telemetry, OEE, and SPC/Cpk; you can override its state (Running / Idle / Down / PM) and run MES Track-In / Track-Out.
  • Modes & scenarios: Visitor / Engineer / Research modes; six simulation scenarios (Baseline, Overlay Drift, Etch RF Down, Scheduled PM, Cascade Fail, AI-APC).
  • More: Follow a Wafer tracks one wafer's journey; YMS Wafer Map shows defect patterns; Roof shows the rooftop HVAC; Section cuts the building.
  • 导览：点顶部 Tour 按钮开始 5 站电影式导览（宏观 → 光刻 → 刻蚀 → AMHS → B1），可 Previous / Next / Skip，播完一轮自动停止；首次访问有 3 步新手气泡引导。
  • 视角：相机预设 L1 / B1 / Dual / Explode；图层开关 AMHS / FFU / Airflow / Heatmap / X-Ray（X-Ray 可看 MEP 管网透视图，按介质过滤）。
  • 看设备：点左侧设备列表或 3D 场景中的机台，右侧 inspector 显示尺寸（SEMI E72）、重量、SECS/GEM 遥测、OEE、SPC/Cpk；还可以手动切换设备状态（Running / Idle / Down / PM）和 MES Track-In/Track-Out。
  • 模式与场景：Visitor / Engineer / Research 三种模式；6 个仿真场景（Baseline、Overlay Drift、Etch RF Down、Scheduled PM、Cascade Fail、AI-APC）。
  • 其他：Follow a wafer 跟踪一片晶圆的旅程；YMS Wafer Map 看缺陷分布；Roof 看屋顶 HVAC；Section 剖切建筑。

3nm Mega Fab Concept 3nm 概念超级晶圆厂

twin-mega.html
Conceptual Layout Model (50,000 WSPM · 3,000 Tools) 概念布局推演示意模型（50,000 WSPM · 3,000 台机台）

A macro-scale 3D conceptual layout model illustrating an advanced 3nm FinFET dual-phase Mega Fab (Phase 1 West Hall 25k WSPM + Phase 2 East Hall 25k WSPM expansion, connected by a central automated AMHS Skybridge).

Important Engineering Note: This model is strictly a Conceptual Layout Model, not an exact 1:1 physical digital twin. Rendering 3,000 production machines with pipe-level CAD sub-assemblies inside a web browser on everyday office laptops or small screens is physically and computationally impractical. Instead, it employs hardware-accelerated GPU instancing (THREE.InstancedMesh) to macroscopically simulate cleanroom equipment density, 10 process bay distributions, and a continuous 13-closed-loop AMHS monorail logistics network. 宏观尺度的 3D 概念布局推演示意模型，展现一座先进制程 3nm FinFET 双期超级晶圆厂（西厂区一期 25k WSPM + 东厂区二期 25k WSPM 扩产线，中央由高速 AMHS 天桥连廊相通）。

重要工程定位说明：本模型严格定位为概念示意模型（Conceptual Layout Model），而非 1:1 细节复刻的施工级孪生。在常规屏幕与普通电脑浏览器中对 3,000 台机台做高细节 1:1 管线级精细建模既不现实也没有必要。因此采用 GPU 硬件级几何实例化（InstancedMesh）架构，核心用于宏观直观演示 3,000 台超大体量机台的洁净室空间密度、10 大工艺湾分区以及 13 条闭环天车（AMHS OHT）高速物料流向。

  • Dual-phase full-flow lines: Phase 1 (West Hall) and Phase 2 (East Hall) each house 1,500 tools across 10 identical process bays (EUV/DUV Litho, FinFET Gate Etch, Contact Etch, ALD High-k, Low-k CVD, Copper PVD, CMP, Implant/Anneal, Metrology/Clean). Both are complete 25k WSPM lines.
  • Inter-phase Skybridge & AMHS loops: 13 closed-loop monorail tracks with 86 active OHT pods, including a 938m Grand Skybridge Highway transferring FOUPs between Phase 1 and 2 for dynamic load balancing and tool redundancy.
  • Heatmap modes & live inspection: Cleanroom realistic, SEMI E10 state, OEM vendor spectrum, process category, and thermal/power density. Clicking any of the 3,000 machines opens live specs, SEMI E10 state, and wafer lot telemetry.
  • Cinematic tour & radar: 5-stop guided macro tour plus a live 2D radar minimap tracking all 86 circulating OHT vehicles in real time.
  • 双期全流程产线：一期（西厂）与二期（东厂）各拥有 1,500 台机台，涵盖 10 个完整工艺湾（EUV/DUV 光刻、鳍线刻蚀、双大马士革、ALD/CVD 薄膜、PVD 铜互连、CMP 研磨、离子注入/退火、清洗量测），均为 25k WSPM 全流程产线。
  • 跨期天桥与全闭环天车物流：13 条全闭环单向轨道、86 辆在轨巡航 OHT 天车，包含跨越 50 米天桥的 938 米高速主干环线，实现跨厂区负载均衡与机台冗余备份。
  • 热力图模式与机台探查：支持洁净室原貌、SEMI E10 状态、OEM 厂商谱系、工艺大类与功耗热力 5 种着色；任意点击 3,000 台机台中的任一台均可唤起实时遥测与工件批次（Lot ID）详情。
  • 巡检导览与微型雷达：5 站宏观巡检导览，右上角雷达动态呈现全厂 86 辆天车流转轨迹。

OSAT Packaging & Test Twin OSAT 封测厂 Twin

twin-osat.html
The back-end packaging & test plant 后道封装测试厂

"From wafer to shipment": a dual-hall facility (west ISO 7–8 Assembly hall + east Final Test hall) with 86 tools across an 11-step flow — dicing → die attach → wire bond → flip chip → molding → singulation → bumping/RDL → wafer probe → ATE test → test handler → AOI inspection. "从晶圆到出货"：双大厅厂房（西侧 ISO 7–8 组装厅 + 东侧终测厅），86 台设备、11 个工艺步：切割 → 贴片 → 键合 → 倒装 → 塑封 → 切筋 → 凸块/RDL → 探针测试 → ATE 测试 → 测试分选 → AOI 检测。

  • Process tour: click Tour and the camera walks the 11 steps in order; the FLOW bar (1–11) at the bottom also jumps directly.
  • Filtering: the bottom chips filter tools by process step (scrollable on small screens); the left directory searches by vendor, model, or process.
  • Inspect a tool: clicking a card opens the inspector — note its honesty labels: most tools' 3D shapes are procedural category placeholder shells, the panel states "Dimensions pending verification" explicitly, and gives the data source with a citation link.
  • Logistics simulation: 4 AMRs and 2 technician trolleys move through the plant, labeled "Logistics Illustrative" — a parametric animation, not a true scheduling simulation.
  • 工艺流程导览：点 Tour，镜头按 11 步顺序走完整个封测流程；底部 FLOW 条（1–11）也可以直接点跳。
  • 筛选：底部 chips 按工艺步筛选设备（小屏幕可横滑）；左侧目录可搜索厂商/型号/工艺。
  • 看设备：点设备卡打开 inspector——注意它的诚实标注：大多数设备的 3D 外形是"程序化类别占位壳"，面板会明确写 Dimensions pending verification（尺寸待核实），并给出数据来源与引用链接。
  • 物流仿真：厂内有 4 台 AMR + 2 台人工推车在跑动画，标注为"物流仿真为示意"——是参数化示意，不是真实调度仿真。

04 How to judge whether data is trustworthy

SemiconFab's core discipline: every data point carries a provenance tier

04 怎么看数据可不可信

SemiconFab 的核心纪律是每个数据都标来源等级，不盲目追求虚假精度

SemiconFab's core discipline: every data point carries a provenance tier. There are five:

Tier Meaning & Evidentiary Standard
Vendor Confirmed From the OEM's official datasheet, product manual, or direct vendor specification.
Independent Verified Cross-checked and corroborated against independent third-party sources (technical papers, fab reports, patents).
Community Corroborated Multiple community submissions that agree and corroborate one another.
Inferred Inferred from trade press, used-equipment market listings, fab photos, and other indirect sources.
Synthetic Placeholder or simulated data (e.g. the MIMAC-2026 benchmark's synthetic fab).
🔍
Tier badges appear throughout the catalog, the compare page, and both twins' inspectors. If a tool is marked Draft / Dimensions pending, its 3D shape is only a category placeholder shell — trust the panel's text and source links, not measurements taken off the model.

SemiconFab 的核心纪律是每个数据都标来源等级，共 5 档：

等级 (Tier) 含义与证据标准
Vendor Confirmed 厂商确认 来自 OEM 官方规格书、产品手册或直接厂商授权规格。
Independent Verified 独立核实 经第三方独立来源（技术文献、晶圆厂建设纪要、专利档案）交叉验证。
Community Corroborated 社区佐证 社区多方提交并相互印证的数据。
Inferred 推断 由半导体行业贸易媒体、二手设备市场挂牌信息、现场照片等间接来源推断。
Synthetic 合成 占位或模拟生成数据（例如 MIMAC-2026 调度基准中的合成逻辑晶圆厂）。
🔍
在目录、对比页和双子的 inspector 里，等级徽章无处不在。看到 Draft / Dimensions pending 的设备，说明它的 3D 外形只是类别占位壳——参数以面板里的文字与来源链接为准，不要量图当真。

05 Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Licensing, system requirements, data accuracy, bulk access, and navigation

05 常见问题（FAQ）

开源协议、配置要求、数据准确性、批量使用与导航说明

Q1. Do I need an account or payment?

No. Everything runs in the browser. The project is licensed under PolyForm Noncommercial: the source is available, free for education, academic research, and personal use; commercial use requires a license (info@semiconfab.org).

Q2. What do I need to run it?

A modern desktop browser (Chrome / Edge / Firefox / Safari) with WebGL. The 3D twin pages are heavy; on older hardware, turn off layers such as Heatmap / X-Ray first.

Q3. What if data is incomplete or wrong?

Of the 253 catalog records, 9 are fully L1-verified today; the rest are drafts of varying completeness — deliberately, and labeled as such. Contribute via the Add Equipment form, or report errors to corrections@semiconfab.org.

Q4. Can I use the equipment data in bulk?

The catalog lives in the GitHub repository (jinjiefan2020/SemiconFab) as catalog.json; its structure is documented on the Data Standard page. Please respect the license and carry the provenance tiers with the data.

Q5. Why is there no link from the Fab Twin to the OSAT Twin?

By the site owner's design: the two twins are independent entry points — switch between them from the "3D Twins" menu in the top navigation.

Q6. Why is the 3nm Mega Fab labeled as a "Conceptual Layout Model" rather than a full 1:1 physical twin?

A leading-edge 50k WSPM Mega Fab houses ~3,000 production machines and hundreds of kilometers of subfab piping. Building a 1:1 photorealistic physical digital twin with millimeter-level sub-assemblies for 3,000 tools to run smoothly in a web browser on everyday laptops or office screens is technically impractical and visually overwhelming. The Mega Fab page uses GPU instanced rendering (THREE.InstancedMesh) as a Conceptual Layout Model to intuitively demonstrate the macroscopic spatial scale, process bay distribution, and automated AMHS closed-loop logistics of a multi-billion-dollar dual-phase manufacturing complex. For micro-level 1:1 equipment inspection and subfab utility details, please explore the dedicated 300mm Fab Twin (121 tools) and OSAT Twin (86 tools).

Q1. 需要注册或付费吗？

不需要。全部功能在浏览器内运行。项目采用 PolyForm Noncommercial 许可证：源码公开，免费用于教育、学术研究与个人用途；商业使用需联系授权（info@semiconfab.org）。

Q2. 对电脑/浏览器有什么要求？

现代桌面浏览器（Chrome / Edge / Firefox / Safari）+ WebGL 即可。3D 双子页面较重，老设备上可先关掉 Heatmap / X-Ray 等图层。

Q3. 数据不全/有错怎么办？

目录 253 条里目前 9 条是 L1 完整核实，其余为不同程度的草稿——这是有意为之的诚实状态。用 Add Equipment 表单补充，或通过 corrections@semiconfab.org 纠错。

Q4. 我想批量使用设备数据。

目录数据在 GitHub 仓库（jinjiefan2020/SemiconFab）的 catalog.json 中，结构见「数据标准」页；引用时请遵守许可证并注明来源等级。

Q5. 为什么 Fab Twin 里没有通往 OSAT Twin 的链接？

站长有意为之：两个双子是独立入口，请从顶部导航「数字孪生」菜单切换。

Q6. 为什么 3nm Mega Fab 明确标定为“概念布局模型（Conceptual Layout Model）”，而不是做完全 1:1 的精细孪生？

一座月产 50,000 片晶圆的先进制程超级晶圆厂容纳约 3,000 台超大型设备与数百公里动力管网。如果在普通电脑浏览器或小屏幕上对 3,000 台设备全部进行 1:1 施工级精细建模，不仅会造成硬件崩溃，视觉上也极其杂乱繁重，在工程上是不现实的。因此该页面采用硬件级 InstancedMesh 架构定位为概念布局推演示意模型（Conceptual Layout Model），旨在让用户宏观直观感知超级晶圆厂的宏大空间尺度、双期工艺湾分区与 AMHS 闭环天车物流体系。若需要体验 1:1 精细度机台与动力层（Subfab）管网，请进入专门的 300mm 洁净室孪生（121台）与 OSAT 封测厂孪生（86台）。
