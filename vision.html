Why Does Semiconductor Manufacturing Need an Open Initiative?

Chip design has welcomed open-source waves like RISC-V and OpenROAD, yet wafer manufacturing remains constrained by three structural dilemmas:

1. Proprietary Monopolies & Prohibitive Costs

Commercial Fab software suites (Applied SmartFactory, Siemens Opcenter, KLA Klarity) cost tens of millions per site with closed proprietary protocols, creating stubborn data silos.

2. Severe Talent Gap in Universities & Research

Students and researchers rarely access real 300mm production systems. The absence of open, sanitized simulation sandboxes leaves the industry chronically short of interdisciplinary talent.

3. Scarcity of Open Reference Benchmarks

AI researchers seeking to advance FDC diagnostics, OHT dispatching, or yield root-cause analysis lack compliant, physics-consistent benchmarks. The Open Initiative creates this golden standard.

4. Winner-take-all ecosystem

Every equipment category is owned by one or two giants — lithography, etch, inspection alike. The moat isn't just technology; it's the cost of evaluation. No fab can risk production to qualify a challenger's tool, so challengers can never earn the track record they'd need. An open equipment catalog plus reference-grade simulation attacks exactly this wall.

CORE PRINCIPLES

Design Principles

Four foundational values guiding our open-architecture engineering approach.

Open knowledge

Fab know-how is among the most secretive in industry. An open equipment catalog and scheduling benchmark give students, researchers, and the curious a place to start.

Honesty over precision

A teaching and research tool, not an industrial twin. Every record carries a source tier; gaps are labeled, synthetic data is marked synthetic.

Source-available

PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0, free for education and personal use. (Deliberately not 'open source': the noncommercial term matters.)

A healthier ecosystem

An open catalog lets challengers sit side by side with incumbents under one schema; reference simulation gives new tools a credible first step; subfab vendors get their first public storefront.

THE SIX FOUNDATIONAL PILLARS

The 6 Full-Stack Digital Twin Pillars

A genuine fab digital twin is never just a 3D showroom—it is a comprehensive architecture integrating spatial geometry, operations research, industrial protocols, process physics, streaming data, and open ecosystems.

01

Physical & CAD Spatial Twin

Elevating generic bounding boxes into verified SEMI E72 footprints and BIM LOD300 models. Faithfully reconstructing internal machine anatomy, cleanroom microenvironments, and underground Subfab utility runs.

Modular Tool Anatomy EFEM mini-environments, 300mm Load Ports with FOUP docking pins, vacuum transfer chambers (VTC), robotic arms, and cluster chamber viewports.
Subfab Basement & Piping Topology Edwards dry vacuum pumps, SMC chillers, abatement scrubbers, and color-coded utility pipelines for UPW, specialty gases, vacuum, and chemical exhaust.
ISO Class 1/3 Cleanroom Environment Ceiling 1200×600mm FFU arrays, 0.45 m/s laminar downflow velocity fields, perforated raised floor tiles (30% open ratio), and positive pressure control.
Photolithography Amber Bay Optical Zone UV-blocking 589nm amber yellow lighting zone to prevent photoresist premature exposure.

02

MES & AMHS Logistics DES Engine

Advanced semiconductor manufacturing is a massively re-entrant discrete logistics network. Powered by Discrete Event Simulation (DES) to route 25-wafer FOUPs through hundreds of processing bays.

800+ Step Re-entrant Routing Tree Litho, Etch, Implant, Deposition, and CMP cycling, realistically simulating repeat tool visits, queue buildup, and tool contention.
Strict Inter-Step Q-Time Windows Enforcing 2-4 hour limits between pre-clean and dielectric deposition to prevent native oxide and molecular contamination, with dynamic priority preemption.
AMHS OHT Anti-Collision & Deadlock Prevention Time-Space Network Flow routing algorithms for overhead transport vehicles to eliminate main track congestion and gridlock.
Stocker Automation & Dispatch Optimization Integration with Google OR-Tools for dispatching heuristics (FIFO, SPT, Critical Ratio) to maximize bottleneck tool OEE.
// MES & Logistics Engine Stack
DES Kernel:SimPy / Salabim (Python) / Rust-DES
Operations Solver:Google OR-Tools / OptaPlanner
Carrier Standard:SEMI E87 CMS / SEMI E40 PJM
Commercial Suite:Siemens Tecnomatix / FlexSim / Muratec
Time Sync:Discrete-Event Virtual Clock Engine
03

Connectivity & Virtual GEM300 Tool Layer

Full implementation of SEMI 300mm communication standards. Open Virtual Tool Simulators allow end-to-end testing of MES commands, state reporting, and alarms without physical hardware.

Full GEM300 Standard Suite SEMI E40 (Process Job), E87 (Carrier Management), E90 (Substrate Tracking), and E94 (Control Job Management).
High-Frequency EDA / Interface A Channel High-bandwidth sensor telemetry channel compliant with SEMI E157/E164, decoupled from transactional MES traffic.
Open Virtual Tool Simulator Bidirectional S1F1 handshakes, S2F41 remote commands, S6F11 event reports with SEMI E10 state machines (Run/Idle/Down/PM).
OPC UA & Industrial Gateway Adapters Open62541 and Eclipse Milo bridges connecting tool controllers to modern enterprise IoT buses.
// Industrial Protocol Stack
Transport Layer:HSMS (SEMI E37) / SECS-II (E5)

Open Protocol Lib:secsgem (Python) / Open-SECS
Commercial Suite:PEER Group / Cimetrix CIM300
State Definition:SEMI E10 RAM (Reliability, Availability)
04

Process Physics & YMS Engine

Yield is the lifeblood of the fab. Transitioning from macroscopic empirical formulas to compact surrogate physics, industrial STDF/KLARF stream parsing, and spatial commonality root cause analysis (RCA).

Compact Surrogate Physics Metamodels Within-wafer non-uniformity (WIWNU) for etch/CVD, chamber seasoning drift, and lithography overlay 3-sigma residuals.
STDF V4 & KLARF Industrial Ingestion High-throughput binary parsers extracting ATE wafer test bins, defect coordinate maps, and parametric parametric distributions.
Commonality Analysis & Root Cause Traceback Reverse indexing shared tools and chamber IDs for wafer lots exhibiting ring or localized cluster defect patterns.
Spatial Defect Adder Analysis Subtracting baseline defect maps between consecutive inspection points to isolate tool-induced particle adders.
// Process & Yield Engine Stack
Test Data Standard:SEMI STDF V4 / STDF-V4-Spec
Open Parser:PySTDF (Python) / rust-stdf (Rust)
Defect Benchmark:WM-811K (811,472 Wafers)
Commercial Suite:KLA Klarity / PDF Solutions Exensio
Yield Model:Poisson / Murphy / Seeds Negative Binomial
05

Industrial High-Throughput Data Fabric

A 300mm fab generates tens of terabytes of telemetry daily. Transcending standalone relational databases with a modern streaming bus, time-series telemetry store, and columnar OLAP analytics fabric.

Sub-Second Industrial Message Bus Apache Kafka and MQTT ingesting tens of thousands of S6F11 events, tool state changes, and OHT telemetry points.
Time-Series Telemetry Store TimescaleDB and InfluxDB storing high-frequency RF power, mass flow controller (MFC) curves, chuck temperatures, and chamber pressures.
High-Throughput OLAP Analytics ClickHouse and DuckDB executing sub-second aggregations and multi-dimensional drill-downs across billions of wafer test points.
Streaming SPC & Nelson Rule Engine Real-time Cp/Cpk process capability calculations and Shewhart charts triggering automated alarms and tool interlocks.

PHASE 1 IMPLEMENTATION STRATEGY

Phase 1 Strategy: Utmost Morphological Realism

In Phase 1, our objective is morphological fidelity rather than full closed-loop control: complete visual modularity, authentic industrial proportions, and convincing realism. Achieving professional engineering credibility at minimal compute and infrastructure cost.

1. Modular Machine Feature Decomposition

Transcending monolithic cubes with lightweight procedural assemblies:

  • EFEM Mini-Environment: Antistatic glass viewports and SCARA transfer arms
  • Load Ports: 2-4 angled docking stations with kinematic pins and tri-color status lights
  • Cluster Architecture: Central vacuum transfer chamber with 4-6 reaction chambers
  • Peripheral Details: Overhead exhaust flanges, cantilevered touchscreens, and SEMI E10 light towers
Cost: Minimal (client-side 3D) · Fidelity: Leaps ahead

2. Cleanroom Spatial Atmosphere & Depth

Recreating the unmistakable aura of an advanced semiconductor cleanroom:

  • Perforated Floor Tiles: 30% open ratio airflow grid with recessed sub-floor seams
  • Ceiling FFU Array: Tightly packed HEPA filter louvers and shadowless high-lumen white lighting
  • Photolithography Amber Glow: Distinctive 589nm amber yellow illumination in litho bays
  • Subfab Utility Racks: Pipe runs color-coded for UPW blue, specialty gas orange, and vacuum gray
Spatial Depth: Immersive · Brand Recognition: Instant

3. Procedural Mechanical Kinematics

Choreographing realistic mechanical actions using client-side timelines without complex dispatch servers:

  • OHT Drop Sequences: Vehicle halts $\rightarrow$ hoist cables descend $\rightarrow$ FOUP latches onto Load Port
  • Wafer Transfer Arm: Load Port opens, vacuum end-effector transfers 300mm wafer into loadlock
  • Chamber Glow: Etch chamber viewports pulse with soft violet/magenta plasma illumination
  • Andon Towers: Steady green running, blinking blue standby, breathing amber recipe change
Visual Authenticity: Uncompromising · User Experience: Dynamic

4. Physics-Consistent Telemetry Sparklines

Generating telemetry curves that adhere strictly to real semiconductor process physics:

  • Etch RF Power Signature: Pumpdown vacuum drop $\rightarrow$ ignition strike spike $\rightarrow$ steady etch $\rightarrow$ EPD step
  • Thermal Inertia: Electrostatic chuck temperature exhibiting authentic first-order lag response
  • Shewhart Control Charts: Statistical UCL/LCL bounds with occasional Nelson rule alert points
  • SEMI E10 OEE Breakdown: Live dynamic pie allocation across Productive, Standby, Down, and PM
Domain Inspection: Flawless · Industrial Rigor: Maximal

Phase 1 Core Value: Winning Trust with High-Velocity Agility Zero Heavy Backend

By perfecting the external modular form and industrial detail first, we build visual consensus and industry awareness. In Phases 2 and 3, we progressively integrate discrete simulation (DES) and GEM300 protocols upon this trusted foundation.

Four-Phase Evolution Roadmap

A pragmatic progression from authentic visual form to fully closed-loop industrial digital twins.

PHASE 1

High-Fidelity Visual Form ✓ Completed

Modular tool anatomy (EFEM/Chambers), perforated flooring, amber litho bay, Subfab utility racks, OHT kinematics, and physics-consistent FDC waveforms.

✓ Pure frontend · Low cost · High impact
PHASE 2

Discrete-Event Simulation (DES) ✓ Completed

Introducing SimPy/Rust DES engines: re-entrant 800+ step lot routing, strict Q-Time residency windows, and deadlock-free OHT logistics scheduling. (delivered via the MIMAC-2026 benchmark and in-browser simulation)

→ Physics-consistent fab logistics
PHASE 3

GEM300 Virtual Tool Sandbox

Open SECS/GEM servers simulating SEMI E40/E87/E90/E94 protocols, recipe execution, and high-speed EDA telemetry streams.

→ Complete industrial interoperability
PHASE 4

Closed-Loop Ecosystem Benchmark

STDF/KLARF stream parsing, open 28nm benchmark dataset release, one-click Docker sandbox, and open plugin ecosystem.

→ Growing open ecosystem
