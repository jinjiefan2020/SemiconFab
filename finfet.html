FinFET 3D Tri-Gate Transistor Fabrication Process: 10-Step Industrial Engineering Breakdown

This interactive digital twin details the complete industrial fabrication sequence of advanced 14nm / 7nm FinFET (Fin Field-Effect Transistor) semiconductors using the Replacement Metal Gate (RMG / Gate-Last) process flow.

10-Step FinFET Semiconductor Fabrication Sequence

  1. Step 1: Silicon Substrate & Well Implantation (P-Well/N-Well)

    Single-crystal silicon wafer preparation and high-energy ion implantation creating deep retrograde wells, establishing the electrical foundation for CMOS pairs.

    Equipment: High-Energy Ion Implanter (Applied Materials Varian VIISTA / Axcelis Purion H). Materials: P-Type Single-Crystal Silicon Substrate, Boron (p-well) / Phosphorus (n-well) dopants.

  2. Step 2: SADP Fin Pitch Splitting & Silicon Dry Etch

    Using Self-Aligned Double Patterning (SADP) to double the line density beyond optical lithography limits, etching sub-10nm wide vertical silicon fins with vertical sidewall angles (>89°).

    Equipment: High-Density Conductor ICP Reactive Ion Etcher (Lam Research Kiyo / TEL Tactras, HBr/Cl2/O2 chemistry). Materials: Single-crystal Si Fin Array (Width: ~8nm, Height: ~55nm, Pitch: ~30nm).

  3. Step 3: Shallow Trench Isolation (STI) Oxide Fill & CMP

    Gap-free filling of inter-fin trenches with flowable CVD (FCVD) silicon dioxide, followed by chemical mechanical planarization down to the fin hardmask.

    Equipment: Flowable CVD (AMAT Producer) + Chemical Mechanical Polisher (Applied Materials Reflexion LK). Materials: SiO2 Dielectric Oxide.

  4. Step 4: Selective STI Oxide Recess (Fin Height Release)

    High-precision isotropic wet chemical etching to uniformly recess the STI oxide layer by ~40nm, unmasking the vertical active silicon fin conduction channel.

    Equipment: Single-Wafer Wet Cleaning Station (TEL CELLESTA / SCREEN SU-3200). Materials: Dilute HF / Siconi chemical vapor etching.

  5. Step 5: Sacrificial Dummy Poly-Si Gate Patterning

    Depositing sacrificial polysilicon and SiN hardmask, followed by 193nm Immersion / EUV lithography and dry plasma etch to pattern the dummy gate traversing the 3D fins.

    Equipment: ArFi Immersion Scanner (ASML Twinscan NXT) + Poly RIE Etcher. Materials: Polysilicon (~70nm) + Si3N4 Hardmask (~30nm).

  6. Step 6: Low-k Inner Gate Spacer Deposition & Anisotropic RIE

    Conformal atomic layer deposition of low-dielectric constant SiOCN/SiBCN thin films followed by directional dry etching to create self-aligned protective sidewall spacers.

    Equipment: Plasma-Enhanced ALD (Lam Research Vector) + Spacer RIE Etcher. Materials: Low-k SiOCN / SiBCN (k < 4.5, Thickness: ~6nm).

  7. Step 7: Source/Drain Cavity Recess & Embedded SiGe/Si:C Epitaxy

    In-situ anisotropic dry etching of source/drain cavities followed by selective epitaxial growth of diamond-faceted faceted SiGe (pFET) inducing strong uniaxial compressive channel strain.

    Equipment: UHVCVD Epitaxy Reactor (Applied Materials Centura Epi / ASM Intrepid). Materials: Embedded Boron-doped SiGe (pFET) / Phosphorus-doped Si:C (nFET).

  8. Step 8: Interlayer Dielectric (ILD0) Oxide Fill & CMP

    Depositing dense ILD0 oxide over the entire wafer, followed by chemical mechanical planarization stopped precisely at the top of the sacrificial dummy polysilicon gate.

    Equipment: PECVD System + Advanced CMP System (AMAT Reflexion LK). Materials: High-density plasma SiO2 Oxide.

  9. Step 9: Dummy Poly Gate Selective Strip (RMG Cavity Formation)

    Selective wet chemical etching using hot ammonia / TMAH to cleanly dissolve the sacrificial polysilicon gate without damaging the active vertical fin channels.

    Equipment: Automated Wet Etch Station. Materials: TMAH / NH4OH wet chemistry, selectivity > 200:1 over SiO2/SiN.

  10. Step 10: High-k Dielectric (HfO2) & Metal Gate (HKMG) ALD Metallization

    Atomic layer deposition of sub-nanometer interfacial oxide, HfO2 high-k gate dielectric, tuned work-function metal layers (TiN/TaN/TiAl), and low-resistance Tungsten metal gate fill.

    Equipment: High-k Metal Gate ALD Cluster (TEL Trias / Applied Materials Olympia). Materials: HfO2 High-k (k~25) + TiN/TaN Work-Function Metal + Tungsten (W) fill.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) on FinFET Fabrication

Why does FinFET replace traditional planar MOSFET?
FinFET features a 3D conducting fin wrapped on three sides by the gate electrode, providing supreme electrostatic control that suppresses short-channel leakage and enables low-voltage operation below 22nm.
What is the Replacement Metal Gate (RMG) process?
RMG (Gate-Last) fabricates a temporary sacrificial polysilicon dummy gate, performs high-temperature (>1000°C) source/drain annealing, and replaces the dummy gate with sensitive High-k dielectrics and work-function metals at low temperatures.
How does embedded SiGe epitaxy improve transistor speed?
SiGe has a larger lattice spacing than silicon. Growing embedded SiGe in source and drain applies strong uniaxial compressive mechanical stress along the channel, increasing hole mobility by over 50% in pMOS transistors.
